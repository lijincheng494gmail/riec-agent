# Failure history

Negative and failed experiments define the sufficiency boundary of RIEC-Agent.

- Early one-shot experiments showed that a genuinely untouched final holdout was already strong; complex governance added no useful advantage in that regime.
- Several provider and protocol stages failed closed because transport, completeness or prespecified confirmation conditions were not met.
- The final Qwen v1.6.1 run passed canary checks but returned 1,912/1,920 planned sessions. Eight calls produced zero tokens.
- Consequently the preregistered all-complete status remains `FAIL_CLOSED_BEFORE_C`.
- A later, explicitly authorized available-case adjudication preserved the missingness ledger and tested complete-world and extreme-adverse sensitivities. It did not rewrite the original failure.

These records are not implementation clutter. They show where the method is unnecessary, where provider confirmation is incomplete and why the final claims are narrower than the complete research history.
