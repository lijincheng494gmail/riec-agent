# Claim boundary

## Supported within tested regimes

- A stringent one-shot local procedure can remain calibrated after broad development search.
- Repeated adaptive access to shared holdout evidence can sharply inflate false-claim risk.
- Local identity-aware resets do not automatically compose into system-level error control across agents and claim families.
- Persistent identity-aware accounting improved the tested safety-discovery frontier relative to local resets and blanket global charging.

## Not supported

- universal superiority over every multiple-testing or reusable-holdout method;
- universal behavior across language models or agent architectures;
- evidence that every real AI scientist will generate the tested failure;
- real-world clinical, scientific or economic benefit;
- permission to erase failed runs, missing sessions or negative experiments;
- the claim that the provider experiment was all-complete confirmation.

The 36,000-world independent simulation is the primary calibrated evidence. The LLM-agent study is supporting available-case evidence with eight retained zero-token transport failures.
